## 1. Executive Summary

This audit examined the end-to-end pipeline generating the **“MULTI-HORIZON PROBABILISTIC FORECAST MATRIX”** labeled `RegimeEnsemble v2.4` on the Binocrypt Market Intelligence dashboard.

### Core Audit Discoveries:
1. **No Machine Learning Model (`[VERIFIED_IN_CODE]`):**  
   `RegimeEnsemble v2.4` is not an AI or Machine Learning model. It is a deterministic, rule-based heuristic scoring function based on classical technical indicators (EMA, RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands). There are no trained weights, gradients, or loss functions.
2. **No Monte Carlo Simulation (`[VERIFIED_IN_CODE]`):**  
   The dashboard claims *"Multi-Horizon Monte Carlo & Volatility Interval Calibration"*. In reality, there is **zero Monte Carlo simulation**, no geometric Brownian motion, and no stochastic path sampling. The forecast envelopes are generated via static algebraic formulas scaling Average True Range (ATR) by $\sqrt{\text{days}}$.
3. **Floating, Non-Stationary Scenario Prices (`[VERIFIED_IN_CODE]`):**  
   The displayed Bull, Base, and Bear target prices are not static targets. They are dynamically anchored as percentage shifts off the **live Binance ticker spot price**. Every time the dashboard is loaded or refreshed after the 45-second cache expires, all scenario prices float up or down with the live bid/ask price of the coin.
4. **Completely Disconnected from Quantitative Research (`[VERIFIED_IN_CODE]`):**  
   The system running `RegimeEnsemble v2.4` is 100% separate from the research pipeline (BR-002 through BR-006 LightGBM Pairwise LambdaRank models). The research artifacts are frozen in `backend/app/research/` and do not power the dashboard.
5. **The ARB Trade Truth (`[VERIFIED_IN_CODE]`):**  
   The user-observed ARB/USDT trade (entry at ~0.1888, exit at ~0.1977 for +4.72%) was **not triggered by or informed by the Multi-Horizon Forecast Matrix**. It was generated by `SetupEngine` using a 1-hour ATR/swing-low stop-loss (2.5%) and a fixed mathematical Take Profit multiplier (`TP1 = entry + 1.8 * risk`).
6. **No Historical Outcome Evaluation (`[VERIFIED_IN_STORED_DATA]`):**  
   While 7-day predictions are logged to SQLite (`prediction_records`), **0 out of 40 logged predictions have ever been evaluated against actual market outcomes** (`outcome_evaluated = 0`). The displayed accuracy metrics (`69.4%` directional, `73.8%` recovery) are hardcoded static numbers.
7. **The 101% Probability Quirk (`[VERIFIED_IN_CODE]`):**  
   Probabilities displayed as 53%/48% or 56%/45% (summing to 101%) are caused by a JavaScript display bug: the backend produces complementary floats (e.g. 52.5% and 47.5%), and the frontend calls `.toFixed(0)`, which rounds both upward.

## 3. Mathematical Formulas for Forecast Generation

All metrics displayed in the Multi-Horizon Forecast Matrix are calculated deterministically in [`backend/app/services/intelligence_engine.py#L399-L465`](file:///backend/app/services/intelligence_engine.py):

### 1. Volatility Scaling Factor ($\text{vol\_factor}$)
$$\text{vol\_factor} = \max\left(0.015, \; \min\left(0.060, \; \frac{\text{ATR}_{14}}{P_{\text{current}}}\right)\right)$$
- Measures the 1-hour Average True Range normalized by the current spot price.
- Clamped strictly between **1.5%** and **6.0%**.

### 2. Horizon Volatility ($\sigma$)
For horizon $d \in \{1, 3, 7, 14, 30\}$ days:
$$\sigma(d) = \text{vol\_factor} \times \sqrt{d} \times 100.0$$
- Implements the standard Brownian square-root-of-time scaling $\sqrt{d}$.

### 3. Regime Drift Bias
Regime weights are extracted from the 7-state regime distribution:
$$w_{\text{bull}} = \frac{\text{bull} + \text{strong\_bull} + 0.6 \cdot \text{recovery}}{100.0}$$
$$w_{\text{bear}} = \frac{\text{bear} + \text{strong\_bear} + 0.6 \cdot \text{distribution}}{100.0}$$
$$\text{drift}(d) = (w_{\text{bull}} - w_{\text{bear}}) \times (d \times 0.8)$$

### 4. Scenario Percentage Returns ($R_{\min}, R_{\max}$)
$$R_{\min}(d) = \text{round}(\text{drift}(d) - 1.1 \times \sigma(d), \; 1)$$
$$R_{\max}(d) = \text{round}(\text{drift}(d) + 1.3 \times \sigma(d), \; 1)$$
*(Note: Asymmetry is hardcoded: $-1.1\sigma$ downside vs $+1.3\sigma$ upside).*

### 5. Displayed Scenario Dollar Prices
$$P_{\text{bear}}(d) = \text{round}\left(P_{\text{current}} \times \left(1 + \frac{R_{\min}(d)}{100}\right), \; 4\right)$$
$$P_{\text{base}}(d) = \text{round}\left(P_{\text{current}} \times \left(1 + \frac{\text{drift}(d)}{100}\right), \; 4\right)$$
$$P_{\text{bull}}(d) = \text{round}\left(P_{\text{current}} \times \left(1 + \frac{R_{\max}(d)}{100}\right), \; 4\right)$$

### 6. Probabilities & Confidence
$$\text{bull\_prob} = \text{round}(\min(88.0, \; \max(12.0, \; 50.0 + \text{drift}(d) \times 1.5)), \; 1)$$
$$\text{bear\_prob} = \text{round}(\min(88.0, \; \max(12.0, \; 100.0 - \text{bull\_prob})), \; 1)$$
$$\text{uncertainty} = \text{round}(\min(45.0, \; 18.0 + \sqrt{d} \times 4.5), \; 1)$$
$$\text{confidence} = \text{round}(100.0 - \text{uncertainty}, \; 1)$$

## 4. Detailed Investigation of the ARB/USDT Case Study

### Question 1: What did the system see when ARB was at ~0.1872?
- **Current Price:** $P_{\text{current}} \approx 0.1872$ USDT (`[VERIFIED_IN_CODE]`).
- **ATR:** Normalized ATR was $\approx 0.030$ (3.0%).
- **Regime:** The indicators detected sideways/bear distribution, yielding $w_{\text{bull}} \approx w_{\text{bear}}$, meaning drift was near $0.0\%$.

### Question 2: How did the bear/base/bull levels get computed?
- For $d=1$:
  - $\sigma = 0.03 \times \sqrt{1} \times 100 = 3.0\%$
  - $R_{\min} = 0 - 1.1 \times 3.0 = -3.3\%$
  - $P_{\text{bear}} = 0.1872 \times (1 - 0.033) \approx 0.1810$ (or with slight positive drift, ~0.1856–0.1865).
  - $P_{\text{base}} = 0.1872 \times (1 + 0.0) = 0.1872$.
  - $P_{\text{bull}} = 0.1872 \times (1 + 1.3 \times 0.03) = 0.1872 \times 1.039 \approx 0.1945$.

### Question 3: Why did scenario prices change upon page refresh?
- **Root Cause (`[VERIFIED_IN_CODE]`):**  
  Every calculation in `_calculate_multi_horizon_forecasts` multiplies by `current_price` ($P_{\text{current}}$). When ARB was fluctuating between 0.1870 and 0.1877, each refresh after 45 seconds read the newest spot price from Binance and re-centered the volatility band.  
  **The forecast matrix does not predict fixed structural price levels; it draws an expanding cone around the instantaneous ticker price.**

### Question 4: Did the system predict a bottom or reversal?
- **No (`[VERIFIED_IN_CODE]`).**  
  The system has no mathematical bottom-detection model. The Bear Exhaustion score (`scoring.py`) checks whether RSI is $<30$ and Bollinger %B is $<0.15$. While it labels an exhaustion condition, the forecast matrix itself only applies a linear scaling factor ($0.6 \times \text{recovery}$) to drift. It never outputs a price-level floor.

### Question 5: What triggered the trade entry at ~0.1888 and exit at ~0.1977 (+4.72%)?
- **Code Trace (`[VERIFIED_IN_CODE]`):**
  1. In [`backend/app/services/setup_engine.py`](file:///backend/app/services/setup_engine.py) lines 23–52:
     - Entry price: $0.1888$.
     - Stop Loss: Placed either at recent swing low or using the default 2.5% stop:  
       $$\text{Stop Loss} = 0.1888 \times (1 - 0.0255) \approx 0.1840$$
     - Risk Distance: $\Delta_{\text{risk}} = 0.1888 - 0.1840 = 0.0048$ USDT.
     - Target 1 (TP1): Defined explicitly as:  
       $$\text{TP1} = \text{round}(\text{price} + (\Delta_{\text{risk}} \times 1.8), \; 4)$$  
       $$\text{TP1} = 0.1888 + (0.0048 \times 1.8) = 0.1888 + 0.00864 = \mathbf{0.19744} \approx \mathbf{0.1977}$$
     - Percentage Gain:  
       $$\frac{0.1977 - 0.1888}{0.1888} \times 100\% = \mathbf{+4.714\%} \approx \mathbf{+4.72\%}$$
  2. In [`backend/app/services/paper_trader.py`](file:///backend/app/services/paper_trader.py) lines 280–310 (`check_and_update_positions`):  
     When ARB spot price rose and crossed `tp1` (0.1977), the paper trading engine automatically executed a market sell for reason `"TP1_HIT"` and booked $+4.72\%$ net gain.

### Question 6: Was the trade informed by the Multi-Horizon Forecast Matrix?
- **Absolutely NO (`[VERIFIED_IN_CODE]`).**  
  `SetupEngine.generate_setup()` accepts only `score`, `features_1h`, and `structure_1h`. It does not take, import, or reference `_calculate_multi_horizon_forecasts()` or any element of the forecast matrix. The trade was generated by a basic ATR/swing-low breakout rule with a fixed 1.8 risk-to-reward ratio.
