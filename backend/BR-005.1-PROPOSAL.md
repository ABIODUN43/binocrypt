## 1. Problem Statement & Research Motivation

The BR-005 experiment and its subsequent robustness audit ([`BR-005-ROBUSTNESS-AUDIT.md`](file:///C:/Users/user/.gemini/antigravity/scratch/binocrypt/backend/BR-005-ROBUSTNESS-AUDIT.md)) established three foundational facts:

1. **Cross-Sectional Alpha is Validated:**  
   Model 3 (LightGBM Pairwise LambdaRank) achieved a statistically significant Mean Rank Information Coefficient ($IC = +0.0656$, $t_{HAC} = +2.32$, 95% bootstrap CI strictly positive), strictly monotonic quintile returns ($Q_1 < Q_2 < Q_3 < Q_4 < Q_5$), and positive excess returns over the Equal-Weight benchmark in **4 out of 4 walk-forward folds**.
2. **Defensive Asymmetry:**  
   The ranking model demonstrates remarkable relative strength during down-markets, winning **73.3% of down-market periods** against the benchmark.
3. **The Unhedged Spot Exposure Bottleneck:**  
   Because BR-005 enforced a static, 100% allocation to spot altcoins, the portfolio was fully exposed to broad market liquidation crashes (e.g. Fold 2 bear market, where the market dropped -42.5% and the strategy dropped -36.6%). This beta-driven volatility (~64.5% annualized) held the 14-day Net Sharpe to **+0.53**, leaving the economic gate at `PARTIAL`.

### The Core Architectural Insight:
In quantitative asset management, **relative security selection (alpha)** must be cleanly decoupled from **aggregate market exposure (beta)**. 

Rather than retuning the ranking model (which risks overfitting), **BR-005.1 formulates an independent hypothesis**:  
Can a point-in-time, causal macro-regime exposure overlay dynamically modulate aggregate market exposure ($w_{macro}(t) \in [0.0, 1.0]$) between spot altcoins and risk-free cash (USDT) to eliminate catastrophic market drawdowns while preserving the validated cross-sectional ranking alpha?

## 2. Research Hypothesis & Core Questions

### Primary Formal Hypothesis:
> Applying a point-in-time, causal macro-regime exposure filter $w_{macro}(t) \in [0.0, 1.0]$ to scale total spot equity vs. cash (USDT) will reduce maximum portfolio drawdown by at least 15 percentage points and elevate the out-of-sample Net Sharpe ratio to $\ge 1.00$ after full 0.40% round-trip transaction friction, while preserving cross-sectional alpha during invested periods.

### Key Research Questions:
1. Does conditioning total market exposure on causal Bitcoin and breadth metrics protect the portfolio during severe crypto bear phases (e.g., Fold 2) without whipsawing during normal corrections?
2. Does the cross-sectional ranking alpha of Model 3 LambdaRank remain intact during active risk-on windows?
3. How much turnover friction is introduced by transitions between spot equity and cash, and does the net benefit exceed this friction cost?

## 4. Candidate Macro-Regime Overlays

All overlays are evaluated strictly at the close of day $t$ prior to portfolio rebalancing, utilizing only data available at or before $t$.

### Baseline $E_0$: Unhedged Benchmark (Static 100% Spot Allocation)
- $w_{macro}(t) = 1.0$ at all times. Exactly reproduces the original BR-005 Model 3 $Q_5$ portfolio.

### Overlay $E_1$: Bitcoin Trend Filter (Binary EMA50)
- **Signal:** Daily Close of BTC/USDT at date $t$ relative to its 50-day exponential moving average:
  $$w_{macro}(t) = \begin{cases} 1.0 & \text{if } \text{Close}_{BTC, t} \ge \text{EMA}_{50}(\text{Close}_{BTC})_t \\ 0.0 & \text{if } \text{Close}_{BTC, t} < \text{EMA}_{50}(\text{Close}_{BTC})_t \end{cases}$$
- When $w_{macro}(t) = 0.0$, 100% of portfolio equity is held in USDT.

### Overlay $E_2$: Dual Trend & Breadth Multi-Tier Overlay
- **Signal:** Combines BTC trend status with causal market breadth (fraction of `SBRU_V1` universe trading above their respective 50-day EMAs):
  $$\text{Breadth}(t) = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^N \mathbb{I}(\text{Close}_{i, t} \ge \text{EMA}_{50, i, t})$$
- **Allocation Rule:**
  $$w_{macro}(t) = \begin{cases} 1.00 & \text{if } \text{Close}_{BTC, t} \ge \text{EMA}_{50, BTC, t} \text{ and } \text{Breadth}(t) \ge 0.40 \\ 0.50 & \text{if } \text{Close}_{BTC, t} \ge \text{EMA}_{50, BTC, t} \text{ and } \text{Breadth}(t) < 0.40 \\ 0.00 & \text{if } \text{Close}_{BTC, t} < \text{EMA}_{50, BTC, t} \end{cases}$$

### Overlay $E_3$: Causal 2-State Regime Posterior Filter
- **Signal:** Point-in-time Gaussian HMM fitted strictly on the expanding training window of daily BTC log-returns and 14-day realized volatility.
- Computes causal forward filtered posterior state probabilities at day $t$: $P(\text{State}_t = \text{Bull/LowVol} \mid \mathcal{F}_t)$.
- **Allocation Rule:**
  $$w_{macro}(t) = \begin{cases} 1.0 & \text{if } P(\text{Bull} \mid \mathcal{F}_t) \ge 0.50 \\ 0.0 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$

## 5. Preregistered Validation Gates

To avoid curve-fitting and ensure robust economic legitimacy, an overlay candidate in BR-005.1 must satisfy all four gates on the primary 14-day horizon:

### Gate 1: Drawdown Mitigation Gate
The overlay must reduce maximum drawdown by at least 15.0 percentage points relative to unhedged $E_0$:
$$MaxDD(E_k) \le MaxDD(E_0) - 15.0\% \quad (\le 35.69\%)$$

### Gate 2: Risk-Adjusted Economic Gate (Net of 0.40% Friction)
The overlay must achieve:
$$\text{Net Sharpe Ratio} \ge 1.00$$
and
$$\text{Calmar Ratio} \ge 0.60$$

### Gate 3: Alpha Preservation Gate
During invested periods ($w_{macro}(t) > 0$), the portfolio must maintain a positive gross alpha spread over the Equal-Weight basket ($Q_5 - EW > 0$ on average) and retain an overall batting average $\ge 55.0\%$ across all periods.

### Gate 4: Temporal Consistency Gate
The overlay must improve the risk-adjusted return (or strictly reduce drawdown) in **at least 3 out of the 4** expanding walk-forward folds, specifically avoiding catastrophic loss in Fold 2.
