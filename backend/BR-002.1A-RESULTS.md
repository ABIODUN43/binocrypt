## 7. Key Findings & Scientific Conclusion

1. **Conclusion:** `NO_SIGNIFICANT_DIFFERENCE`
2. **Empirical Return Distribution:** Unconditional depressed states exhibit negative drift relative to the general universe baseline (-1.25% on 14D, -4.97% on 30D), with 95% block bootstrap confidence interval [-1.98%, -0.48%].
3. **Barrier Asymmetry:** In depressed states, downside stops are triggered with substantially higher frequency than upside expansions (70.22% vs 21.12% for Barrier A; 66.37% vs 15.60% for Barrier B).
4. **Research Implication for BR-002.1B & BR-003:** An unconditional depressed-state filter is NOT a positive edge on its own. Buying simply because an asset is down -35% and oversold is systematically inferior to the market average unless conditioned on structural accumulation and forward path probability (BR-003). Advancing to predictive modeling requires structural conditioning rather than naive dip-buying.
